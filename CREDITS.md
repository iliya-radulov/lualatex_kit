# Credits

This project was motivated by Manuel Pégourié-Gonnard's **"A Guide to LuaLaTeX"** (2010), originally distributed under the GNU Free Documentation License. That guide was one of the earliest practical introductions to LuaLaTeX for newcomers, and it remains one of the few resources written with students in mind.

The materials in this repository are a 2026 rewrite for current TeX Live, focused on the needs of computational linguistics students working with Romance languages. The structure, examples, package choices and prose are new; the original 2010 guide served as inspiration rather than as a source of reused text.

- Original author of the 2010 guide: **Manuel Pégourié-Gonnard**
